Delete your Guardians of the Mist account

Guardians of the Mist by Majorion Studios

You can delete your account and your cloud save at any time, in the game or by email. You do not need to reinstall the game to do it.

Option 1: in the game

  1. Open Guardians of the Mist.
  2. On the title screen, tap Settings (the gear), then Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Delete my account.

Your account and cloud save are deleted from our server straight away. You need an internet connection.

Option 2: by email

Write to majorionstudios@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and include one of these:

  • the Account ID shown in the game under Settings > Account, or
  • the email address you linked to the account (if you added one). Please write from that address, so we know the request comes from the account's owner.

We may ask a short question to confirm the account is yours. Parents and guardians can make this request for their child.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: its ID, sign-in keys and linked email address.
  • Your cloud save: heroes, items, currencies, progress, the save summary and the device model name.

What may be kept

  • Purchase records. Google Play keeps its own records of purchases made through it, under Google's privacy policy. If we hold records of purchases for tax, refund or legal reasons, we keep only what those duties require, for as long as they require.
  • Technical logs kept by our hosting provider for security (such as IP addresses) are removed automatically after a short period.
  • Progress on your phone is not touched by deleting the account. To remove it, uninstall the game or clear its data in your phone's settings. If Android backup is on, a copy of the game's files may also be in your Google account backup, which you control.

We keep no other copy of your data.

How long it takes

In-game deletion is immediate. Email requests are completed within 30 days, and we reply to confirm.

Note: deletion cannot be undone. Gems and items bought with real money are part of the deleted save and cannot be restored afterwards.
