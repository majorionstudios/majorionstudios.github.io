Guardians of the Mist

A family-friendly action RPG for Android, set in a blocky voxel world above a sea of mist.

Coming soon to Google Play

The lights are going out

Our world floats on a sea of mist. Take up the last lantern, choose your hero and fight through the fog to bring your little sister Mira home. Move with your left thumb, attack and cast with your right, roll out of a boss's swing and grab the loot it drops. Each map takes a few minutes and ends with a boss.

What's inside

Five campaigns, 50 stages

Drowned marshes, red canyons, frozen walls, floating temples and lava fields. Every stage ends with a boss, then Veteran, Nightmare and Nightmare+ tiers.

The Forge

Gear in six rarities, Common to Mythic. Craft, fuse three into one, upgrade to +10 and reroll a stat.

The Lighthouse

Climb 100 floors each month, a boss on every 10th, then take on Hard mode.

Come back each day

Three daily contracts, a bonus chest and a 28-day calendar that waits for you and never resets.

Your look

Capes, wings and class outfits. Looks only: no stats, and never sold by chance.

Play offline

Campaigns, the Forge, the Lighthouse and daily rewards work without a connection. Online, your progress is saved to the cloud.

Four heroes

Vanguard

Sword and shield: charges and whirlwinds on the front line.

Lancer

Spear and javelins, traps, decoys and a hawk.

Mistcaller

Orbs, lightning and ice from a safe distance.

Bonebinder

Raises skeletons and a peat shambler to fight at your side.
