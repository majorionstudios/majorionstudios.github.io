Privacy Policy: Guardians of the Mist

Effective date: 17 November 2026

App: Guardians of the Mist (Android, package com.majorionstudios.guardians)

In short: the game keeps a copy of your game progress in the cloud so you do not lose it. It does this with an anonymous account; we never ask for your name. Connecting a Google account is optional. Ads are optional, rewarded ads only, and every ad request is marked as directed to children, so ads are not personalised. We do not sell your data. You can delete your account at any time.

1. Who we are

Guardians of the Mist is made by Majorion Studios ("we", "us"). For any privacy question or request, write to majorionstudios@gmail.com.

3. Third parties

  • Supabase hosts our account and database service. It stores the account and save data above on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes. (Supabase privacy policy)
  • Google Sign-In: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google shows its own account screen and confirms to us which Google account you picked. We never see your Google password. (Google privacy policy)
  • Google AdMob shows the optional rewarded ads. Every ad request is marked as directed to children and under the age of consent, with ad content limited to the "G" (general audiences) rating. For such requests Google serves only non-personalised ads. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob software in the game sends Google information such as your IP address (which can indicate your general area, not your precise location), device and app information, ad interactions and performance data. (How Google uses information from ads, Google privacy policy)
  • Google Play handles payments for optional gem purchases. We never see your card or bank details; Google tells us only that a purchase was made so we can deliver it. (Google privacy policy)
  • Android backup: if backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the game's files on your phone to your own Google account. This is controlled by you and Google, not by us.

We do not sell your data, share it with data brokers, or use it for advertising profiles. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

4. Children

Guardians of the Mist is a family-friendly game and children play it. We designed it to collect as little as possible:

  • The game works without asking for any personal details; the cloud save uses an anonymous account.
  • All ad requests are marked as child-directed, so ads are non-personalised and family-safe for every player, whatever their age.
  • There is no chat, no friends list and nothing a player writes is shown to other players.
  • Connecting a Google account is optional; the game can be played as a guest. For a child's Google account managed by a parent (for example with Google Family Link), the parent's settings decide whether the child can use it to sign in to apps.
  • Purchases go through Google Play, where parents can require approval for every purchase.

We follow the principles of the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the children's rules of the EU and UK GDPR. A parent or guardian can ask us at any time to see or delete their child's data: write to majorionstudios@gmail.com (see section 6).

5. How long we keep data

  • Account, cloud save and Google account details: until you delete the account.
  • Game activity events: 400 days, then deleted automatically (or when you delete the account).
  • Technical logs: for a short period set by our hosting provider, then deleted.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed to deliver purchases, handle refunds and meet tax and legal duties.
  • Data on your phone: until you uninstall the game or clear its data.

6. Deleting your data

  • In the game: Settings > Account > Delete account. This deletes your account and your cloud save from our server straight away. Progress on your phone stays until you uninstall the game.
  • Without the game: follow the steps on our account deletion page, or email majorionstudios@gmail.com. We complete requests within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct or receive a copy of your data, or object to how it is used. Email us and we will help.

7. Security

All data travels encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Database rules let each account read and change only its own save. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the data we hold small and protect it with care.

8. International transfers

Our service providers may store and process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where the law requires it, these transfers are covered by the providers' standard safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. If a change is important, we will also tell you in the game.
